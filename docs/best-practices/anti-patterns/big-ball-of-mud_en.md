# Big Ball of Mud
## Overview

Big Ball of Mud is an anti-pattern that describes systems with no clear architecture or modularity, where ad-hoc changes have created a tangled codebase. This hinders maintainability, slows development, and makes refactoring risky and expensive. To mitigate, introduce modular boundaries, incremental refactoring, and automated tests to safely peel off layers.

## Why it's a problem
It hinders maintainability, slows development, and makes refactoring risky and expensive.
