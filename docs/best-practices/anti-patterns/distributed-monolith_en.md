# Distributed Monolith
## Overview

A Distributed Monolith is an anti-pattern where a microservices-style system is tightly coupled and cannot be deployed or scaled independently. This loses many benefits of microservices while adding distributed complexity, making deployments risky and slow. To mitigate, define clear bounded contexts, improve service autonomy, and refactor to decouple deployments and data ownership.

## Why it's a problem
