# Chatty Services
## Overview

Chatty Services are an anti-pattern where services require many back-and-forth calls to accomplish simple tasks, increasing latency and coupling. This leads to high latency, network overhead, and brittle interactions between services. To mitigate, consolidate calls, use bulk APIs, or redesign boundaries to reduce chattiness.

## Why it's a problem
Leads to high latency, network overhead, and brittle interactions between services.

## Mitigation
Consolidate calls, use bulk APIs, or redesign boundaries to reduce chattiness.
